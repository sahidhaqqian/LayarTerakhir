# Phase 1 audio sources

Actual recorded performances, processed offline. No DreadOut files or dialogue TTS used for these chapter sounds.

- **Lift** — YCbCr, [source](https://opengameart.org/content/generator-loop), [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Filters: `atrim=0:2.5,afade=t=in:d=0.1,afade=t=out:st=2:d=0.5,alimiter=limit=0.7`.
- **Halogen** — themightyglider, [source](https://opengameart.org/content/electric-buzz), [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Filters: `atrim=0:1.5,alimiter=limit=0.7`.
- **Kunti** — AlucardsBride, [source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cry_of_woman.ogg), [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Filters: `highpass=f=110,lowpass=f=6500,acompressor=threshold=0.14:ratio=2,afade=t=in:d=0.3,afade=t=out:st=25.5:d=0.8`.
- **Manifest** — Darsycho, [source](https://opengameart.org/content/monster-snarls), [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Filters: `asetrate=44100*0.82,aresample=32000,highpass=f=45,alimiter=limit=0.8`.
- **Tuyul** — AntumDeluge, [source](https://opengameart.org/content/evil-laugh), [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Filters: `asetrate=44100*1.55,aresample=32000,highpass=f=160,alimiter=limit=0.8`.

Foot/Impact/Glass/Chain/Seal/Drip/Tape derive from the existing, credited project foley bank (see Audit/asset-credits.md). Human dialogue/EVP acting remains a production requirement; subtitle text is preserved rather than presented as final recorded acting.
