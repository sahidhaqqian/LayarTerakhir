# Interlude street references (Wikimedia Commons, viewed Oct 2026)

All photos CC-licensed on Commons; used as visual direction only (no pixels
or 3D derivatives copied into the game). 3D assets in-game are Kenney CC0 +
project-licensed scooter/vendor models (see `Audit/interlude-street-*-source.json`).

1. `File:Warteg pinggir jalan.jpg` — roadside warteg: blue tarp awning angled
   over the front, full-width painted banner facade (WARUNG TEGAL BAROKAH),
   open serving area, stools, gerobak parked alongside.
   → Warteg Bahari: banner facade, angled blue tarp, stools/bench, menu board.
2. `File:Menu Warteg di Jakarta.JPG` — etalase interior: 2 glass shelves with
   15–25 stainless bowls/plates of lauk, rice cooker + dandang behind,
   hanging rentengan strips, krupuk jars.
   → Etalase with 2 tiers × 8 Kenney food bowls, rice cooker, dandang,
   rentengan strips, interior lamp.
3. `File:Batagor vendor 3.jpg` — gerobak anatomy: wood body ~1×2 m, glass
   case with painted text reading correctly from outside, counter clutter
   (sauce bucket, wok, cig pack), roof on poles.
   → Batagor/Siomay carts: glass panels, painted sign, bucket, wok, stools.
4. `File:Barisan angkot.jpg` — red minibuses queued on a narrow road,
   motorcycles filtering alongside, dense parking at the road edge.
   → Sedan + angkot van parked at the road edge, 2 moving traffic scooters
   in their own lanes (alley itself stays motor-width only).
5. `File:Gang di Jalan Kelinci Pasar Baru.JPG` — narrow gang: facades at 0 m
   setback, zinc kanopi over the walkway, wires crossing overhead, doors
   opening directly onto the lane, AC units, roster vents.
   → Zone A: 3.5 m alley, kanopi, catenary wires, doors/AC/roster/house nos.
6. `File:Human Interest Pedagang - Kehangatan di Balik Gerobak.jpg` —
   vendor cart with yellow-frame roof, stools around, street context.
   → Cart roof/trim colors, stool scatter.

Owner references: Ref A (narrow alley, parked motors both sides, weathered
walls) → Zone A proportions/parking; Ref B (wider road, 2–3 story mixed
shophouse facades, overhead cables, parked cars/bikes) → Zone B massing,
wires, sedan/angkot placement.
