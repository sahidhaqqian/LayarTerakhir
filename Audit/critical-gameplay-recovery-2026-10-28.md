# Gameplay and visual recovery — 28 October 2026

The recovery is implemented. The final targeted selection passed **124/124** cases with no failures or skips. A graphics-enabled repeat passed **5/5**, producing **21 Unity Editor PlayMode frames**. A subsequently strengthened phone-call placement assertion passed **1/1**. These repeats overlap the 124-case selection; they are not additional unique tests. No Windows build or R2 upload was performed.

## Confirmed causes and repairs

| Area | Cause traced in the repository | Correction |
|---|---|---|
| Giant phone menu | `c131d6ab` changed RMB release from restoring/stowing the phone to leaving Home raised. `landscapeFromStowed` still cleared only when the handset lowered completely. The menu therefore retained a 1.90 landscape aspect indefinitely. | End the aiming hold explicitly and clear its rise-only layout flag. A hold begun from the pocket returns to Home; a hold begun in Journal or another menu restores that menu. Tab/F take precedence over held RMB. Portrait width follows the canvas aspect and physical handset proportions, and the grip render target/projection follows viewport resizing. |
| Focus and input | Alt+Tab shared the Tab binding with the phone. Aim state depended on a held mouse button and did not handle lost focus/releases. Cursor state was also used implicitly as control state. | Suppress gameplay shortcuts while Alt is held. Focus loss cancels aim, restores the menu, clears transient presentation state and waits for a neutral RMB before rearming. Movement/look respect application focus, the first recapture mouse delta is discarded, and aiming has an explicit shared state. Cursor intent is recorded separately from native OS readback. The existing pause/resume flow remains in place. |
| Inward knees and ankle popping | `936951b1` transferred source knee positions relative to the pelvis despite Raka having a wider anatomical stance. It also reset the complete foot quaternion to bind orientation at a toe-height threshold. `c131d6ab` then re-solved every standing gait against a fixed lateral pole in `LateUpdate`, replacing the authored knee plane. | Retarget bend directions relative to each thigh–ankle chain, stabilize near-straight chains, retain sourced foot/toe orientation, and fit the actual sole vertically when needed. Remove the fixed runtime knee override. Transplant only 96 leg rotation channels across Walk, Run, PhoneWalk and PhoneRun into the current FBX. |
| Hand/arm contact | The recent call solver targeted the wrist itself at an assumed ear point while preserving a generic world hand orientation. That ignored the handset grip offset. | Derive handset and skull contact frames from the actual meshes in the editor, then solve the wrist using the handset offset/orientation. Exclude hanging head-weighted hair/hood geometry from the skull bounds. Store seven metadata fields on the existing prefab so shipped meshes remain non-readable and runtime does not read vertices or bone weights. Both imported renderer forms are supported during authoring; the original FBX body and glass both contain skin deformers. |
| Blocked neighborhood | `3630e4ea` placed a facade bay directly outside the gate. A counter sealed the warteg front, a cart overlapped the existing shop/motor row, and the vendor used world coordinates as building-local coordinates. Uniform food scaling produced metre-sized plates/bowls. `2786756e` also added a solid invisible stair interaction box and doubled cylinder heights. | Keep seven useful facade bays and open the direct gate approach. Move the cart/vendor to free frontage, shorten the complete display assembly to provide a real doorway, relocate the obstructing parked motor, place cookware on a supporting prep counter inside the shop, and fit food to actual serving-sized bounds. Make the stair interaction a trigger, correct yard prop dimensions, and lift alley laundry above the walking player. |
| Empty moving motorcycles | Traffic cloned parked motorcycles without attaching riders. | Attach the existing character rig and Riding clip to each of the two traffic motorcycles, fit the real saddle/handlebar/footboard contacts, retain the authored head direction during torso fitting, and freeze the fitted pose. Rider and motorcycle share one moving root. Traffic colliders are disabled immediately. |
| Toy-like cars | The Kenney sedan/van had stylized proportions and colliders independent of their rendered size. | Replace that pair with one full-scale, licensed sedan, keeping the street less crowded. Preserve the imported FBX basis beneath a placement root and derive the collider from the actual model. The source is optimized to 12,000 triangles with material roles for paint, glazing, tyres, lamps and metal. |

The concurrent `eea7812a` fix that keeps the alley group off during the night prologue was fast-forwarded into the recovery worktree and retained. Campaign, story, checkpoint/save schema and Phase Review launcher code were not rewritten.

## Remaining human verification

Physical desktop Alt+Tab, native cursor capture, multi-monitor/fullscreen resizing and Windows input-device behavior remain unverified. The batch GameView reported native cursor `None` while the controller correctly requested locking; that observation is recorded explicitly and is not reported as a native cursor pass. Focus callbacks and Alt+Tab key combinations were injected into the real runtime systems.

The captured walk samples show separated knees, coherent foot progression and support, but long play sessions, all blending/contact circumstances, subjective hand/arm naturalness and character-camera framing still need designer playtesting. The traffic uses the existing character/Riding asset rather than newly commissioned unique rider art. All campaign phases were not manually played through. Intel Iris Xe frame time, memory behavior and final visual acceptance are not certified by software rendering or test counts.
