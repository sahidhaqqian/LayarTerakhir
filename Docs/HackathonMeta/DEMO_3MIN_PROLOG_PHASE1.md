## 4. Arahan editing & audio

1. **Ritme:** potongan 3–6 detik; chase 1–2 detik; tahan shot loker minimal 3 detik penuh tanpa musik.
2. **Audio mix:** VO paling depan → dialog in-game → SFX kunci (shutter, dinamo, langkah Nyonya) → ambience → musik paling belakang. Target loudness ± −14 LUFS, peak ≤ −1 dBTP.
3. **Jeda wajib:** ±1 detik hening sebelum Nyonya muncul (01:24-an) dan sebelum logo (02:58).
4. **Subtitle:** Inggris burned-in untuk semua dialog Indonesia (Damar, Raka, chat penting). Font sans tebal, outline hitam, maksimal 2 baris.
5. **Teks layar:** hanya yang tercantum di tabel; jangan tambah klaim fitur.
6. **Warna:** jangan grade ulang footage; samakan brightness antar-shot saja agar transisi Prolog→Phase 1 tidak jomplang.
7. **Ekspor:** `LayarTerakhir_MetaHackathon_Demo_3min.mp4`, H.264, 1080p, bitrate 16–20 Mbps, audio AAC 320 kbps. Simpan juga master tanpa subtitle (clean) untuk cadangan.

## 5. Teks submission (siap copy-paste)

- **Judul:** Layar Terakhir — The Last Stream
- **Tagline:** Your phone is your only weapon. Every photo costs battery. The audience is always watching.
- **Deskripsi singkat (±80 kata):** Layar Terakhir is a first-person Indonesian horror game about Raka, a broke livestreamer hired by a mysterious viewer to spend one night in the haunted Rumah Atmaja, searching for his missing cameraman. Photograph hidden evidence through the MataBatin phone camera, manage a battery that every action drains, hide from Nyonya in the basement — then continue the investigation into Rusunawa Griya Asri, where a Deduction Journal, EVP recordings, and the Genderuwo Arang demand entirely new rules of survival.
- **Scope demo:** Prologue (Kamar Kos, Kampung Melati, Rumah Atmaja) and Phase 1 (Rusunawa Griya Asri) from the current playable build. The campaign continues beyond this demo.
- **Tech:** Unity (URP), C#, Windows build; first-person + shoulder camera, smartphone AR/EVP/Blitz systems, deduction journal, stealth/chase AI.
- **Kontrol yang ditampilkan:** WASD gerak, tahan klik kanan angkat kamera HP, LMB foto/Blitz, E interaksi, Ctrl jongkok, Space vault/lompat, Tab HP.
