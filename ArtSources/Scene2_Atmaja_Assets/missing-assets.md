These shipped assets exceed comfortable raw budgets for the Intel Iris Xe / 8 GB target. Decimate or use partial subsets (see per-asset notes in asset-manifest.json):

- **modular_electricity_poles** - 200,610 triangles. Roadside, outside the boundary wall. 200k tris raw - use one pole and decimate hard, or rebuild the pole from the included profile pieces.
- **dead_tree_trunk** - 101,802 triangles. Across the side corridor at head height for a 1.6-1.8 m player; clear one crouch-height gap. 101k tris raw; decimate or use as the hero piece.
- **root_cluster_01** - 225,261 triangles. At the trunk base against the house side. 225k tris raw - decimate heavily or use only the front shell.
